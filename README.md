### 02 — Arcwell — Governed Agentic CRM

**Agentic AI · Governance · Human-in-the-Loop**

A governed AI layer for CRM workflows where agents can reason over authorized context and propose actions without being trusted to execute sensitive operations directly.

**Core idea**

```text
Authorized Context
       ↓
      RAG
       ↓
 LangGraph Agent
       ↓
Structured Proposal
       ↓
  Policy Engine
    ↙      ↘
 Allow    Approval Required
              ↓
            Human
              ↓
    Controlled Execution
              ↓
          Audit Trail
```

**Focus**

`LangGraph` · `FastAPI` · `Qdrant` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis` · `HITL` · `RBAC`

> AI proposes. Policies authorize. Humans approve sensitive actions.

### 05 — AI Sales Assistant

**LangGraph · Sales Automation · n8n**

An AI-assisted sales platform that qualifies leads, gathers structured information and automates follow-up workflows while keeping business scoring deterministic.

**Key engineering decision**

The LLM helps understand conversations and extract structured lead information, but **does not directly control the lead score**.

```text
Conversation
     ↓
Input Validation
     ↓
Safety Check
     ↓
LangGraph / LLM
     ↓
Structured Lead Data
     ↓
Deterministic Scoring
     ↓
Human Handoff
     ↓
n8n Automation
```

**Automation workflows include**

- Lead Capture
- AI Qualification
- Hot Lead Alert
- Follow-up
- Appointment Booking
- Meeting Reminder
- Global Error Handling

**Stack**

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `LangGraph` · `OpenAI` · `PostgreSQL` · `n8n` · `React`
